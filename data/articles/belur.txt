After years of wandering the world, Swami Vivekananda finally established a permanent headquarters for the Ramakrishna Math and Mission on the banks of the Ganges.

On December 9, 1898, he carried the urn containing the sacred relics of his master, Sri Ramakrishna, on his own shoulder to the new site. He consecrated the ground saying, "The Master will be installed here today, and I pray to him that from here he may inspire the whole world for the welfare of all."

He designed the main temple himself to be a symbol of harmony. If you look closely at the architecture of Belur Math, you will see it resembles a Temple, a Mosque, and a Church all at once, reflecting his message of the universality of religions.
